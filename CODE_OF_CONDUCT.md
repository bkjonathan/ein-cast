# Code of conduct

EinCast is a small project for people sharing media at home. Everyone who takes part in it, in issues,
pull requests, discussions and any other space that belongs to the project, is asked to make it a place
where a beginner and an expert are both treated well.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient. Assume good faith, and say so when you are unsure about someone's meaning.
- Disagree with ideas, not with people. Say what you think is wrong and why.
- Help newcomers. A question that looks basic to you was hard once.
- Respect that English is not everyone's first language, and that Burmese speakers are part of this
  project. Writing in either is welcome.
- Take responsibility for mistakes, and let others take theirs.

## What we do not accept

- Harassment, insults, threats, or demeaning comments about someone's identity, background, ability,
  age, religion, gender, nationality or language.
- Sexual language or images, and unwelcome attention.
- Publishing other people's private information (including their library, addresses or log files)
  without permission.
- Asking for help getting pirated content. EinCast plays files you already have.
- Anything else a reasonable person would find hostile in a project like this.

## What happens

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close issues, lock threads and block people to keep the
space safe. They will usually say why. A first problem will normally get a warning; a serious one may
not.

## Reporting

Tell the maintainer, [@bkjonathan](https://github.com/bkjonathan), privately: use GitHub's
**Report content** on the comment, or the private "Report a vulnerability" form described in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) if there is no other way. Reports are kept confidential. If the
problem is with the maintainer, say so in the report and say what you would like to happen.

This text is our own short wording, in the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
